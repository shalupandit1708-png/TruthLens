Important Limitation

TruthLens is a machine-learning-based fake-news classification system. It identifies patterns learned from its training dataset.

The prediction is not an independent verification of whether a claim is factually true or false.

Therefore, the confidence score should be interpreted as the model's classification confidence, not as a percentage of factual truth.
